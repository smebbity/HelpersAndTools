📝 Game Notes

The Amnesiac Bounty Harvest Hot Pot

The Amnesiac was going to make a hot pot for town and is looking for seasonal ingredients and they need help to collect crops for it.

Their ability is this:

  1. The Cauldron Keeper
    Each night, choose a Beggar to gather hot pot ingredients. You learn the quality score of their harvest.
  2. They can send one person out per night.
  3. That person chooses 2 people to go begging at.
  4. Depending on the crops given/people visited the amnesiac is given a number (of which are townsfolk or outsiders).
  5. The amnesiac's ability still works when/if they die.

The Amnesiac Appoints the beggar for the night

  • Wake the Amnesiac.
  • They say the name of the beggar.
  • Tell the amnesiac to go back to sleep and wait.

The beggar Enters & Chooses

  • Move the beggar into your private voice channel.
  • Tell them: "You have been chosen to go Bounty Begging tonight. Name two players you want to visit."
  • The beggar names Player 1 and Player 2.

Player 1

  • Move Player 1 into the channel with you and the beggar.
  • Say: "Player 1, you hear a knock on your door. [Beggar] is here Bounty Begging! Please offer one of your remaining crops."
  • Roleplay: Let them interact/roleplay. Once Player 1 names their crop, record it, and move them back to Town Square. The beggar stays in the channel.

Player 2

  • Move Player 2 into the channel.
  • Say: "Player 2, [Beggar] has arrived at your door Bounty Begging! Please offer one of your remaining seasonal crops."
  • Roleplay: Let them interact/roleplay. Once Player 2 names their crop, record it, and move Player 2 back to Town Square.

Dismiss the beggar

  • You can now briefly chat with the beggar or move them straight back to the Town Square to sleep.

Calculation

  • Calculate the total score (Summer/Autumn = 1 point, Winter/Spring = 0 points).
  • Wake the Amnesiac (or DM them): "You get a [0, 1, or 2]."

The Hot Pot

As soon as they are very warm or hot (or spot on) announce the crops that have been recorded and put into the hot pot in town.
